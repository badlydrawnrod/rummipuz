      How to play

      A group is three or four tiles with the same number and different colours. A run is at least three consecutive numbers of one colour, ordered from low to high.

      Split, extend, and rearrange rows to use every tile. Matching copies are separate tiles. A row can be temporarily invalid while you work; to finish, every row must be valid and your rack must be empty.

      Numbers run from 1 to 13 without wrapping. There are no jokers or opening-point requirement.
